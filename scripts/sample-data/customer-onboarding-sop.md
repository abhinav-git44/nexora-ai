# Customer Onboarding SOP (Sample)

## Kickoff
The customer success manager schedules a kickoff call within 2 business days of contract signature.
The kickoff agenda covers goals, success metrics, stakeholders and the implementation timeline.

## Implementation
Standard implementations complete within 21 days. Enterprise implementations may take up to 45 days.
Data migration requires a signed data processing agreement before any customer data is imported.

## Handover
After go-live, the account moves to steady-state support. A health check is scheduled at day 30 and day 90.
Escalations during onboarding go to the Head of Customer Success.
