# IT Helpdesk Guide (Sample)

## Password resets
Employees can reset their own password through the self-service portal. Passwords expire every 90 days and must be at least 12 characters.

## Laptop requests
New laptops are provisioned within 5 business days of a manager-approved request. The standard model is a 14-inch ultrabook with 16 GB RAM.

## VPN
The VPN is mandatory when accessing internal systems from public networks. VPN issues should be logged as P2 tickets.
