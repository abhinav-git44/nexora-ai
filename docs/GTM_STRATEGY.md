# Go-to-Market Strategy

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
| Attribute | ICP |
|---|---|
| Company | 10–200 employees; SaaS/tech, digital agencies, professional services, D2C operations teams |
| Geography | India (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad) first; then UK/US/SEA English-first SMBs |
| Trigger events | New ops/HR hire; growing past ~25 people; first SOC2/DPDP review; repeated "where is the policy" questions |
| Champion | Ops Manager / Chief of Staff / HR Manager |
| Economic buyer | Founder/CEO or COO |
| Disqualifiers | Requires on-prem; <5 employees; no documented processes at all |

## 2. Positioning statement
For operations leaders at growing companies who are the human search engine for "how do we…", NEXORA AI is the governed AI
operations workspace that answers from your own documents with sources, turns conversations into owned tasks and runs approval-gated
workflows — with the cost and outcomes visible. Unlike AI add-ons priced per seat or automation tools without a knowledge layer,
NEXORA puts knowledge, agents, workflows and measurement under one permission model at a team price.

## 3. Channels (ordered by expected CAC)
| Channel | Tactic | Target metric |
|---|---|---|
| Product-led (lowest CAC) | Reverse trial, templates, shareable conversations, hosted forms carry a subtle "Powered by NEXORA" | Viral coefficient, signup→activation |
| Content/SEO | "SOP template + AI" guides, HR policy FAQ templates, India-specific (DPDP, GST) operations content | Organic signups |
| Communities | Ops/Chief-of-Staff communities, SaaSBoomi/Indian SaaS founder groups, HR communities | Qualified signups |
| Partnerships | Fractional COOs/ops consultants (multi-org use), n8n community templates, accounting/HR consultants | Partner-sourced orgs |
| Launches | Product Hunt, Hacker News "Show HN" (self-host angle) | Spike + backlinks |
| Paid (targeted) | LinkedIn to Ops/CoS titles at 10–50 staff companies — **Pro offers only** (Starter LTV/CAC < 2) | CAC ≤ $550 |
| Founder-led sales | Design partners and inbound Enterprise | 10 paid design partners |

## 4. Activation strategy
Time-to-first-value target < 10 min: 5-step checklist, sample documents (E3), starter prompts (E2), agent/workflow templates,
notes→tasks as a no-setup "aha", email nudges at 24h/72h for orgs without an outcome.

## 5. Product-led growth loops
1. **Collaboration loop:** invite teammate → shared conversations/approvals → more questions → more outcomes.
2. **Artifact loop:** hosted forms and reports seen by external people.
3. **Template loop:** users publish workflow recipes (V2 marketplace).
4. **Usage → upgrade loop:** limits at 80% trigger in-app upgrade with value summary ("You completed 132 outcomes this month").

## 6. Retention strategy
Weekly digest (outcomes, time saved *estimate*, pending approvals), workflows that run on schedules (habitual value without logging
in), knowledge freshness alerts (V2), health score = outcomes/week + active members + workflows succeeding; CS outreach when health
drops for paid orgs.

## 7. Expansion strategy
Seat band upgrades, Pro-only data intelligence & audit logs, credit packs, departmental spread (HR → Sales → Support templates),
Enterprise conversion for SSO/custom limits.

every 2 weeks. Jira for sprint execution (`docs/product/backlog.csv` import), Confluence space mirrors `/docs`.
