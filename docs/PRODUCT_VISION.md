# Product Vision — NEXORA AI

> **One intelligent workspace. Infinite business possibilities.**

## Vision
Every growing business runs on the same invisible operating system: policies in documents nobody can find, processes that live
in a few people's heads, spreadsheets that answer last quarter's question, and dozens of small hand-offs that someone has to remember.
NEXORA's vision is a workspace where that operating system becomes *queryable, automatable and measurable* — where any team member can
ask the business a question and get a sourced answer, and where recurring work runs itself with a human approving only what matters.

## Mission
Reduce repetitive operational work, make organisational knowledge accessible to everyone who is allowed to see it, and turn business
information into actions that are owned, dated and tracked.

## What NEXORA is (and is not)
| NEXORA is | NEXORA is not |
|---|---|
| A **workspace for operations teams** combining knowledge, AI assistance, agents, workflows, data questions and tasks under one permission model | A general-purpose chatbot or a replacement for a CRM/ERP |
| **Grounded**: answers cite the documents they come from, and say "not found" instead of guessing | A model playground with unconstrained outputs |
| **Governed**: tenant isolation, role-based document access, approvals for side-effects, audit logs, spend controls | An autonomous agent swarm acting without oversight |
| **Measured**: tracks completed outcomes and AI cost per outcome | A tool judged by "number of prompts" |

## The 3-year picture (aspirational, not a commitment)
1. **Year 1 — Trusted answers & first automations.** Teams upload their operating documents, get cited answers, and publish their first
   approval-gated workflows. Success = weekly AI-assisted outcomes per active organisation (North Star).
2. **Year 2 — Connected operations.** Native sync with Google Workspace, Slack, Jira/Confluence and CRMs; agents act across systems with
   scoped credentials and approvals; benchmarked evaluation per customer corpus.
3. **Year 3 — Operations intelligence.** NEXORA proactively spots bottlenecks (SLA drift, stuck tasks, cost anomalies) and proposes
   workflows, with measured time-saved validated by customers.

## Product principles
1. **Grounded over clever** — a cited partial answer beats a fluent wrong one.
2. **Human in the loop where it matters** — automate the boring 90%, ask a human for the risky 10%.
3. **One permission model** — if a person can't open a document, no AI feature will quote it to them.
4. **Cost is a feature** — route each task to the cheapest model that meets the quality bar; show spend per outcome.
5. **Honest by default** — demo mode, estimates and beta integrations are always labelled as such.
