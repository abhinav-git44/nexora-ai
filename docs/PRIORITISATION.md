Full scored backlog (62 stories, Jira-importable): [`product/backlog.csv`](product/backlog.csv) — generated by `scripts/build_backlog.py`.

## 1. Frameworks used and why
| Framework | Used for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **RICE** = Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort | Ordering stories within the backlog | Forces explicit reach and effort; penalises low-confidence bets |
| **ICE** = Impact × Confidence × Ease (each 1–10) ÷ 100 | Quick triage of growth experiments and small items | Cheap to score; good for experiments where reach is unknown |
| **MoSCoW** | Release scoping (MVP vs V1 vs V2) | Communicates commitments to stakeholders |
| **Impact vs effort** | Sanity check against "high RICE but strategically irrelevant" | Visual negotiation tool |
| **Dependency analysis** | Sequencing | Some low-RICE items unblock high-value ones |

Scoring conventions: Reach = organisations affected per quarter (Base-scenario assumption); Impact 0.25 (minimal) – 3 (massive);
Confidence 0.5–1.0; Effort in person-weeks. All scores are judgement calls recorded so they can be challenged.

## 6. What was deliberately excluded and why
| Excluded | Reason |
|---|---|
| Multi-agent orchestration | Single agents with narrow toolsets were more reliable/cheaper for the 8 target jobs (D-007) |
| Building 100+ connectors | n8n integration covers the long tail; we own governance and outcomes |
| Real-time collaborative editing | Low impact on the core jobs; high effort |
| Native mobile apps | Responsive web covers approvals/tasks on mobile |
| Fine-tuned models | No customer data or eval evidence yet that routing + RAG is insufficient |
